<strong>INTRODUCTION</strong>
</p>

A New Way to Learn Japanese

Shirube (しるべ) is a Japanese learning platform built around one simple idea:

A small step in Japanese, every day.

From your first hiragana characters to advanced kanji, Shirube provides an interactive space to practice, track your progress, and stay motivated.

Combining gamification, interactive exercises, spaced repetition, and progression systems, Shirube transforms language learning into a personal journey.
